SurrealDB

Your data. Your rules.

SurrealDB handles authentication and data access. sp00ky compiles supported select permissions into live-query filters, so synced views follow the same rules.

How permissions work ↗
DBSP

Private by architecture.

DBSP updates affected views and writes per-user sync references back to SurrealDB. Keep its ingest endpoints on your private network. Your app connects only to the database.
